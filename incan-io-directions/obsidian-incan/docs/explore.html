Overview

Find your next step

Start with what you want to do. You can follow a learning path, explore a topic, or look up an exact contract.

Write your first program

Install Incan and try your first project, then follow the language book. Coming from Python, Rust or JavaScript? Choose your starting point.

Understand a language feature

Explore the language: types, functions, errors, enums, traits and modules. Guides and exact rules sit together by topic.

Try the reworked traits and capabilities guide, then look up the trait rules.

Work with data and libraries

Browse the standard library. Start with a problem, choose a module, then reach its contract.

For a complete sample, work with dates and times and look up Date. The Date API is an authored preview of future generated documentation, not generator output.

Build, test and deliver

Oven and tools covers project configuration, dependencies, builds, tests and diagnostics. The reworked Oven chapter follows dev.8. Browse all five core topics.

Use Rust or C

Interoperability brings language boundaries, checked bindings and their inspection contracts together.

Change Incan or follow its development

Contribute for the compiler and development workflow. Project for releases, the roadmap, RFCs and design decisions.
